## Full description (max 4000)
Run your own space agency! Build rockets part by part, launch them, reach orbit, dock with a space station, then land on the Moon or on Mars.

Orbital mechanics are realistic (orbits, spheres of influence, atmosphere, planet rotation) in a solar system 100 times smaller than the real one, so everything goes fast.

🚀 BUILD — capsules, tanks, engines, decouplers, fairings, landing gear, stages and radial boosters, with the Δv and thrust of each stage.

🌍 FLY — touch controls designed for phones (throttle, SAS, RCS), maneuver nodes, rendezvous and docking, fuel transfer.

🌙 LEARN BY PLAYING — 8 tutorials, from your first launch to landing on the Moon, and optional flight assists: automatic orbit, maneuver execution, transfers to the Moon and Mars, automatic docking and landing.

🏛️ RELIVE HISTORY — Sputnik 1, Vostok 1, Apollo 11 (Eagle lands while Columbia waits in orbit) and Apollo 13.

Career mode with missions and unlockable parts, or sandbox. Ambient music and sound effects. Cheat menu for fun: rewind 30 seconds, infinite fuel…

Available in English (the language follows your device, and you can switch anytime in the options).

No ads, no in-app purchases, no internet connection, no data collection.
